PSG GAME (ICI C'EST PARIS)

Small group experience

The plan

  • 1. Meet at the PSG store on the Champs Élysées
  • 2. Take the metro, direct with no changes, to an authentic French bistro run by an old friend of mine, for a beer or two before the game
  • 3. An 18 minute walk to the Parc des Princes to attend the game (League 1/Champions League)
  • 4. Before/after the game (depending on the game schedule), lunch or dinner at the bistro
  • 5. Back to the Champs Élysées, where we started
  • Weekend matches and Champions League nights
  • Minimum 4 people
  • Book early: the price depends on the game
